About Me

Meet Sydney 👋

Sydney is a 2026 graduate of the University of Washington, currently working as an Associate Compliance Analyst at Identity Digital Inc. in Bellevue, sitting at the edge of internet infrastructure and the emerging world of AI agents.

She's always been a bit of a paradox: as comfortable building connections over a good conversation as she is untangling complex systems at a desk. That tension isn't something she tries to resolve; it's the thing that drives her.

From coordinating a twelve-person research team on nuclear politics in outer space to presenting data methodology to a team in Mumbai, she's learned that the best outcomes live at the intersection of careful thinking and clear communication.

Whatever room she's in, you can count on Sydney to bring genuine energy and a real desire to get things right.
